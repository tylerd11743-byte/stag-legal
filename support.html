Common Questions

How do I manage or cancel my subscription?
Subscriptions are managed through your Apple ID. Open the Settings app on your device, tap your name, then tap Subscriptions to view, change, or cancel your STAG subscription.

How does the free trial work?
STAG offers a 7-day free trial. You won't be charged until the trial ends, and you can cancel anytime before then through your Apple ID subscription settings.

How do I restore my purchase?
If you've previously subscribed, tap "Restore Purchases" on the subscription screen to restore your access.
